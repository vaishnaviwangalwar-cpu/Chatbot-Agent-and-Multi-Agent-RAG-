Examination Policy — DY Patil University

Semester examinations are held twice a year, at the end of each
semester, according to a schedule published by the academic office at
least 4 weeks in advance.

Students who miss an examination due to documented medical emergencies
or family emergencies may apply for a supplementary examination within
7 days of the missed exam. Applications must be submitted to the
academic office along with supporting documentation.

Students who fail a subject, or who wish to improve a passing grade,
may request an exam retake. Retake requests must be submitted through
the academic office within 2 weeks of results being published, along
with a retake fee of ₹1,500 per subject. Retakes are held during the
following semester's supplementary exam window.

A minimum of 75% attendance in a course is required to be eligible to
sit for that course's semester examination. Students below this
threshold must apply for condonation through their department, which
is granted only in exceptional, documented circumstances.

Grade grievances must be filed within 10 days of results being
published. A re-evaluation fee of ₹500 per subject applies, refunded
if the grade changes as a result of re-evaluation.
