Admissions & Fees Policy — DY Patil University

Undergraduate admissions are based on a combination of entrance exam
score (60% weight) and Class XII marks (40% weight). Applications open
in March each year and close in June; exact dates are published on the
university website annually.

Tuition fees are billed per semester and vary by program. Engineering
programs are ₹1,25,000 per semester, Management programs are ₹95,000
per semester, and Science programs are ₹80,000 per semester. A one-time
admission fee of ₹25,000 is charged in the first semester only.

Students may pay tuition fees in full at the start of the semester, or
in two installments: 60% due at the start of the semester and the
remaining 40% due 8 weeks later. A late payment fee of 2% of the
outstanding amount per week applies after each due date.

Merit scholarships covering 25% to 100% of tuition are available based
on entrance exam rank and are renewed each year contingent on
maintaining a minimum GPA of 7.5 out of 10.

Refunds for withdrawal before the semester start are processed at 90%
of fees paid. Withdrawal within the first 2 weeks of the semester
qualifies for a 50% refund. No refunds are issued after week 2.
