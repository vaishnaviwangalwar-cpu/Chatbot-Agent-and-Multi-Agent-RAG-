Library Policy — DY Patil University

The Central Library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, and
9:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekends and public holidays. During examination
periods (as announced by the academic office), the library extends its
hours to 8:00 AM to 1:00 AM on weekdays.

Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 books at a time, for a
maximum period of 14 days. Postgraduate students may borrow up to 6
books for 21 days. Books can be renewed once online through the
library portal, provided no other student has placed a hold on them.

A late return fee of ₹5 per day per book applies after the due date.
Fees above ₹500 in a semester will result in a temporary suspension of
borrowing privileges until the balance is cleared.

The library also maintains a 24-hour reading room in Block C, separate
from the main lending library, which remains accessible with a valid
student ID at all times, including holidays.

Lost or damaged books must be reported to the library desk within 7
days. Replacement cost is the current market price of the book plus a
processing fee of ₹200.
