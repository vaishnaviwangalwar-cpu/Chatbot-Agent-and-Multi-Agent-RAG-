Hostel Policy — DY Patil University

Hostel accommodation is available to all enrolled full-time students on
a first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability. Priority is
given to first-year students and students residing more than 50 km
from the campus.

Hostel fees are billed per semester and vary by room type: ₹45,000 per
semester for a shared triple room, ₹60,000 for a shared double room,
and ₹85,000 for a single room. Fees must be paid in full within 15
days of the start of each semester; a late payment fee of ₹1,000 per
week applies after this window.

Curfew is 11:00 PM on weekdays and 12:30 AM on weekends. Students
returning after curfew must sign in at the security desk and will
receive a warning; three warnings in a semester may result in a
disciplinary review.

Guests are permitted in common areas only, between 10:00 AM and 8:00
PM, and must be registered with the hostel warden in advance. Overnight
guests are not permitted under any circumstances.

Room changes may be requested through the hostel office and are
processed at the start of each semester only, except in cases of
documented medical necessity.
